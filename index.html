Track what you have read

Your progress is saved in this browser
There is no account and nothing is uploaded. That also means clearing your browser data will erase it, and it will not appear on your phone by itself. The ⇄ button gives you a short code, or a link, that carries your progress to another device.
Reading for the first time?
Turn off Show spoilers before you browse. It swaps every summary for a spoiler-free one.
