# Consolidated Repository Review

**Date:** 2026-07-31
**Classification:** Internal
**Scope:** whole repository. Accuracy, UX, visual design, functionality.
**Method:** six independent parallel reviews (ordering accuracy, canon accuracy, UX, visual design, code, chart extraction). Every claim below was verified by reading or executing code, or by checking canon against Lexicanum, Black Library, Track of Words and The Heresy Reviewed.

**Supersedes `REVIEW-REPORT.md` and `QA-REPORT.md`, both of which are stale and certify a broken state as passing. They should be deleted, not updated.**

---

## Verdict

The repository is a well-structured, genuinely charming prototype whose **skeleton is sound and whose flesh is unreliable**.

What is solid: series numbering (0 errors across I-LIV, P1-P17, SoT 1-8c), author attribution (224/224 correct), anthology membership (158/158 stories in the right volume, none invented, none missing), image path integrity (114/114 resolve), and the curated chronological sequence itself, which exists and is correct.

What is not: the chronological view does not display the chronological order, and the 158 anthology story entries have largely fabricated prose and tags.

Two independent failures, both of which undermine the site's core promise. Neither is hard to fix. One is a one-line change.

## 1. Accuracy: ordering

### 1.1 The chronological view is not chronological

`getSortedBookKeys` (`script.js:4483-4489`) sorts on a `sortOrder` scalar. **Not one of the 224 entries holds a live chronological `sortOrder` value.**

| Cohort | Count | What the comparator actually reads |
|---|---|---|
| Two `sortOrder` declarations in one object literal | 139 | the second wins in all 139 cases |
| One `sortOrder`, value is the anthology index | 19 | anthology index |
| No `sortOrder` at all | 66 | `romanToNumber(number)` = publication index |

Measured impact:

- **223 of 224 books render in the wrong position.** Only `XLIX WOLFSBANE` is correct, at #173.
- Against `ORDERING_DECISIONS.md`, **1 of 213 title-matchable books** is in its documented position.
- **32.7%** of all comparable pairs are inverted (8,024 of 24,531). **62 of 223** adjacent pairs run backwards in time.
- Mean absolute displacement **52.2 places**. 32 books are more than 100 places out. `PROMETHEAN SUN` (001.M31) is displaced 161 places, rendering at #174 after `SLAVES TO DARKNESS` (013.M31).
- `THE LAST CHURCH` (730.M30, the earliest scene in the saga, documented position #1) renders at **#15**.
- `FULGRIM` (Isstvan V, 006.M31) at #5 is immediately followed by `DESCENT OF ANGELS` (820-970.M30, the Lion's childhood), 5,000 years earlier.

### 1.2 Root cause

Commit `0f29f38` created anthology entries with `sortOrder: <anthologyIndex>`. Commit `40f28da` then **inserted** a new `timeline` plus chronological `sortOrder` after the title block instead of editing the existing `sortOrder` further down. The stale value below therefore wins. Commit `84ec900` reordered the object keys correctly and generated `ORDERING_DECISIONS.md`, but never touched the comparator.

### 1.3 The correct order is already in the repo

`Object.keys(bookData)` insertion order matches `ORDERING_DECISIONS.md` at **224/224 positions**. `REVIEW-REPORT.md:262` already asserts this is how the code works. It is not, but it is what it should be.

```js
const chronoIndex = new Map(Object.keys(bookData).map((k, i) => [k, i]));
// case 'chronological':
return keys.sort((a, b) => chronoIndex.get(a) - chronoIndex.get(b));
```

This corrects **223 of 224 positions**. Then delete all 158 `sortOrder` declarations so the bug cannot be reintroduced.

### 1.4 Do not try to repair the `sortOrder` numbers

The `<volume>.<story>` float scheme **cannot work**. `parseFloat("31.10") === 31.1`, so story 10 is indistinguishable from story 1 in any anthology. **8 collision groups covering 17 books**, including `LII.1 MYRIAD` vs `LII.10 THE SOUL, SEVERED`. All three `THE END AND THE DEATH` volumes collapse to `208` because `romanToNumber` strips the `a`/`b`/`c` suffix, so the reading order of the saga's finale rests on sort stability. The dead first values collide too.

### 1.5 Other ordering defects

- **`romanToNumber("X.6")` returns `0.6`, not `10.6`** (`script.js:4445`). It strips the Roman letters. Currently dead code because all dotted entries carry a `sortOrder`, but `CONTRIBUTING.md` invites contributors to add stories and does not mention `sortOrder`, so the next story added sorts to position 1 ahead of the entire archive.
- **"PUBLICATION ORDER" is alphabetical on Roman numerals** (`script.js:4477` uses `localeCompare`). `IX` renders at #5, `V` at #30. 23 of 196 entries out of order. The correct helper sits 14 lines above and is not called.
- **The `Chrono: N` badge is the index in the current sort** (`script.js:4509`). Sort by title and `A DEEPER DARKNESS` is labelled "Chrono: 1" when its true position is 52. The one number a reading-order site is judged on is actively misleading.
- **`THE PHOENICIAN`** has a canonically correct but unparseable `timeline: '566.006.M31'` (`script.js:4048`) and sits at documented position 205, after `THE END AND THE DEATH VOL III`. It is the sole cause of all 138 inversions inside the document's own list.
- **Two entries still hold `timeline: 'Various'`** despite commit `40f28da` claiming to date all of them: `THE LONG NIGHT` (should be ~009-011.M31) and `THE WOLF KING` (~007-008.M31). Both currently sit after the end of the saga.
- **`THE MASTER OF MANKIND`** is dated `004-014.M31` and anchored at range start, placing a late-Heresy novel at position 23 between `THE OUTCAST DEAD` and `PROSPERO BURNS`. The most reader-damaging single placement in the guide. Anchor policy should be narrative present, not range start.
- **`VIRTUES OF THE SONS` precedes `SINS OF THE FATHER`**, but Black Library describes Sins as the prequel to Virtues.

### 1.6 Unresolved editorial contradiction

`ORDERING_DECISIONS.md:15-18` states four principles. **Principle 4 ("Pre-Heresy books placed at end") flatly contradicts principle 1 ("strictly by in-universe timeline")**, and the contradiction is unresolved in the document. The doc puts Primarchs at 208-224 after Siege of Terra at 195-204. The render does the opposite. This needs an owner decision, not a code fix.

### 1.7 Provenance

The `timeline` values for the main novels reproduce **adeptusars.com's chronological guide verbatim**, including its idiosyncratic calls (`Descent of Angels 820.M30-970.M30`, `Legion 001.M31`, `Horus Rising 002.M31-003.M31`). `README.md:192` credits Lexicanum first. The site inherits one community source's editorial judgement and presents it as fact. It conflicts with other reputable chronologies on roughly a dozen books.

### 2.3 `HORUS RISING`'s blurb describes `FALSE GODS`

`script.js:1252-1253` has Horus wounded by the anathame on Davin's moon. That is book 2. *Horus Rising* ends with the Interex debacle on Xenobia. The `blurbSafe` repeats the error, and it is self-inconsistent with *False Gods*' own blurb.

## 3. Functionality and code

### 3.1 One corrupt localStorage value permanently blanks the archive

`script.js:874` calls `JSON.parse` with **no `try/catch`, reached 225 times per render**. Set `horusHeresyProgress` to `{oops` and reload: the header, filters and footer render normally, and **zero book cards appear**. It looks like a styling problem. There is no in-app reset, so recovery requires devtools. `JSON.parse('null')` is a second variant. If `localStorage` access itself throws (Safari with cookies blocked, embedded webviews), the throw happens earlier and **nothing initialises at all**.

`setItem` is likewise unguarded (`:886`), so on quota exhaustion or in Safari private browsing the mark-as-read button is silently inert.

### 3.2 Renaming a book key destroys progress, and it has already happened

`readingProgress` is keyed on `bookData` keys with no schema version and no migration table. **15 keys that existed before commit `0f29f38` are gone at HEAD** (`tales-of-heresy`, `age-of-darkness`, `the-primarchs`, and 12 more). Any user who marked an anthology finished lost it. `updateProgressCounter` only counts keys present in `bookData`, so the loss is invisible. Corroboration: `blurbs-safe.json` still contains all 15 dead keys.

### 3.3 Performance

- **`readingProgress.getStatus()` is called 224 times per render** (`:4510`), each call re-reading and re-parsing localStorage, and it runs **before** the filter `return`s, so the cost is paid for all 224 books even when 3 are shown. Fix is one hoisted line: 225 reads to 1. The boolean migration loop also never persists, so legacy data is re-migrated on every one of those reads forever.
- **18.1 MB of cover art requested on first paint.** 81 distinct covers, used as CSS `background-image` (`:4570`) so `loading="lazy"` cannot apply. 18 files exceed 400 KB. `character-kor-phaeron.jpg` is 1,528 KB rendered into a 200px box, and it is PNG data with a `.jpg` extension, as are three other portraits.
- **`favicon.png` is 313 KB at 700x700**, byte-identical to `images/imperial-aquila.png`, referenced as both `icon` and `apple-touch-icon`. The same 313 KB downloads twice under two filenames for a 16px slot.
- **`styles.css:1` is an `@import` of Google Fonts**, invisible to the preload scanner, so it serialises HTML → CSS → CSS → font. It also contradicts `CONTRIBUTING.md`'s "no external dependencies" rule and sends visitor IPs to Google.
- **Everything initialises on `window.load`** (`:5384`), so the grid waits for all subresources including that 313 KB favicon.
- 224 separate `innerHTML` parses and 224 live listeners per render. Search **is** correctly debounced at 300ms, so this is not per keystroke.

### 3.4 Correctness bugs

- **`makeCharactersClickable` links "Horus" inside "Sons of Horus" (9 entries) and "Horus Heresy" (25 entries)** (`:4818`, `:4851`). Clicking the legion name in `Legion: Sons of Horus` opens the Horus Lupercal modal. The `gi` flag also links lowercase prose.
- **The character modal breaks entirely on Safari before 16.4** (`:4851`). The regex uses lookbehind, unsupported until 16.4, so `new RegExp` throws, `showModal` throws, and **clicking any book card does nothing** with no visible error. `CONTRIBUTING.md` explicitly asks contributors to test Safari.
- **Closing the character modal unlocks scrolling behind the still-open book modal** (`:4907-4910`). The same unguarded `body.style.overflow = ''` pattern recurs in five independent places.
- **Recording progress throws you back to the top of 224 cards.** `generateBookCards()` is called after every status change (`:4773`) and clears `innerHTML`, resetting the scroll of the `.book-display` container. Marking book 90 finished returns you to book 1.
- **Internal sentinels leak into the UI**: selecting "ALL LOYALIST LEGIONS" renders `Showing 132 books featuring __LOYALIST__` (`:4602`).
- **"CLEAR ALL" does not clear the sort order or the spoiler toggle** (`:5024-5030`), though it holds references to both.
- **Five books are unreachable by any legion filter.** `All Legions` and `All Traitor Legions` are excluded from the dropdown and never resolved by the meta-filters.
- **64 of 224 books match neither ALL LOYALIST nor ALL TRAITOR**, including `THE MASTER OF MANKIND`, `MECHANICUM`, `NEMESIS`, `TALLARN` and all 16 Knights-Errant books. `"Audio"` is a format sitting in the legion dropdown.
- **`parseMarkdown` (`:5196-5265`) collapses every list in the document into one `<ul>`** via a non-global greedy regex, and its link substitution would be an HTML injection sink if it ever parsed a real file.
- **`'lion-eljonson'` shadows `'lion-el-jonson'`** only by key order. Reorder the object and every Lion link degrades to a placeholder stub.

### 3.5 Data files are dead weight

No `fetch`, `XMLHttpRequest` or `import()` exists anywhere. `script.js` is the sole authority.

| File | Size | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `complete-characters.json` | 43 KB | byte-identical duplicate of `characterData`, zero differences |
| `lexicanum_characters.json` | 18 KB | superseded, all 32 entries hotlink `wh40k.lexicanum.com` images |
| `blurbs-safe.json` | 22 KB | stale, 15 orphaned keys, 158 current entries absent |

`ORDERING_DECISIONS.md` is a fourth unused data file: the ordering-guide modal renders a hardcoded 3.2 KB fork (`:5270-5347`) that **deletes the 224-book list** and says "see the main dataslate archive above". The button labelled "View Chronological Order Guide" never shows the guide, and the summary asserts principles the grid violates.

Each of these is a trap for a contributor who edits the JSON and sees no effect.

### 3.6 Maintainability

**86% of `script.js` is data** (213 KB `bookData` + 44 KB `characterData` of 298 KB total), and every byte blocks the parser before a card renders. Because the data is a JS object literal, defects like the 139 duplicated `sortOrder` properties are syntactically legal and invisible. `eslint --rule no-dupe-keys` alone would have caught this on the day it landed.

`.gitignore` excludes `*.sh`, `add-covers.js`, `add-legions.js`, `character-data.js` and `character-data-complete.js`, which means **the generators that produced the 213 KB of book data are not in the repository**. That is the deeper cause: the transformation cannot be reproduced or verified by anyone, including its author.

### 3.7 Deployment

`netlify.toml`'s SPA catch-all (`from = "/*" to = "/index.html" status = 200`) is wrong for a multi-asset static site with no router. **Every 404 now returns `index.html` with HTTP 200**, so a mistyped cover path fails silently and search engines index soft-404s. There is **no `[[headers]]` block at all**, so a 28 MB static site ships with no caching policy and no security headers.

No credentials, keys or PII were found.

## 4. UX

### 4.1 Spoiler-free mode protects one surface out of six

The toggle only substitutes `blurbSafe` in the modal body (`:4713`, `:4746`). All 224 entries do have a distinct `blurbSafe`, so the data work was done and the plumbing was not.

1. **The `details` panel is never gated.** **66 entries carry a `Status:` line.** Book II renders `Status: The Corruption Complete` and `Primarch: Horus Lupercal, the Corrupted Warmaster` while the amber "SPOILER-FREE MODE" banner is displayed. Others: `THE MASTER OF MANKIND` → "The Emperor's Vision Dies", `SLAVES TO DARKNESS` → "The Warmaster's Final Corruption".
2. **Character bios are never gated.** **30 of 123 bios contain outcome spoilers.** Sanguinius "ultimately sacrificing himself". Ferrus Manus "killed by his former brother Fulgrim at Isstvan V". `role` and `legion` leak too ("Daemon Prince of Slaanesh").
3. **The safe blurbs link into those bios.** **63 of 224 spoiler-free blurbs contain a one-click link to a spoiler bio**, including `HORUS RISING`.
4. **The ordering-decisions viewer is completely ungated** and is a spoiler table: "006.M31 | Isstvan V Massacre".
5. **Search indexes the spoiler blurb regardless of mode** (`:4542`).
6. **"Appears in" is built from the spoiler blurb** (`:4884`), disclosing presence in late-saga books.

### 4.2 The spoiler preference does not survive a reload, and defaults to off

`index.html:61` ships `checked` and nothing persists it. Only `horusHeresyProgress` and `allegiance` are stored. **A reader who deliberately opts into spoiler-free mode is silently opted back out on their next visit.** For this audience that is the most damaging defect on the site, and a new reader is the one person who cannot recover from a leak.

### 4.3 The site cannot be used without a mouse

`grep` for `aria-`, `role=`, `tabindex` across `index.html`, `script.js` and `styles.css` returns **zero matches**. Cards are bare `div`s with click handlers and no `tabindex` or `keydown` (`:4551-4585`). Character links are bare `span`s. `.focus()` is never called anywhere. All 224 cards and 123 encyclopedia entries are unreachable by keyboard, and no modal has `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, a focus trap or focus restore. The four `✕` buttons have no accessible name.

### 4.4 Information architecture

The flat 224-tile grid mixes 39 novels with 158 short stories and audio dramas as visually identical peers. There is a toggle for Primarchs (17) and Siege of Terra (10) but **nothing for the 158 anthology entries, which are 71% of the list**. No format filter, no "novels only" preset.

Worse, **81 distinct cover images cover 224 entries**: `war-without-end.jpg` appears 21 times, `legacies-of-betrayal.jpg` 19, `eye-of-terra.jpg` 16. And `script.js:4566` prints the literal string `ANTHOLOGY` while **discarding `book.anthology`**, so a card for `MYRIAD` never tells you it lives inside *Heralds of the Siege*. The one fact a reader needs in order to buy it is in the data and withheld from the card.

A first-time reader asking "what do I read next?" is handed a 224-tile wall and cannot reduce it.

### 4.5 No URL state

Zero uses of `history`, `location`, `pushState` or `URLSearchParams`. No book is linkable or shareable, no filter combination is linkable, and the browser Back button exits the site rather than closing a modal, which is a hard failure on Android where Back is the primary dismiss gesture.

### 4.6 Other

- **Progress tracking has no undo, no bulk action, no reset, no export or import**, and no indication that progress lives in this browser only. Correcting a mis-tap on a 224-item list means opening a modal per book.
- **The progress counter treats a four-page short story as one "book"**, giving a denominator of 224. `index.html:27` hardcodes `0/54` and `index.html:7` claims 81.
- **"Appears in" entries have hover styling and are inert `div`s** (`:4897`). The one navigation a reader wants, character to book, is dangled and withheld.
- Touch targets below 44px: close buttons 35px at ≤768, `.clear-search-btn` ~33px, allegiance toggle ~36px at ≤480.
- **`styles.css:161` sets `touch-action: pan-y`, disabling pinch-zoom** on small monospace prose. Fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- `.book-display` is a nested `70vh` scroller (`:573`), so a 224-item catalogue lives in an inner scroll region that competes with the page scroll and with pull-to-refresh.
- No onboarding. Unexplained on first load: the two numbering systems on each cover, what "ANTHOLOGY" means without a parent name, that the mark button cycles three states, what 📜 and 🔒 do.

## 5. Visual design

### 5.1 The traitor theme is unreadable

`styles.css:50-55` forces `.book-title`, `.main-title` and `.filter-label` to `--chaos-red: #8b0000`. **Every book title in Chaos mode renders at 1.53:1.** Half the product's headline feature is illegible.

13 further contrast failures in the traitor theme, including `.timestamp` 1.43:1, `.close-modal` 1.67:1, `.modal-title` 2.28:1. The core error is mapping **both** accent tokens onto reds while keeping the same near-black surfaces. Pure `#ff0000` on near-black tops out at ~5.2:1 and cannot support body text on this palette.

8 further failures in the loyalist theme. Two compounding bugs: `.quote-attribution` sits inside a parent with `opacity: 0.8` so opacities multiply to 0.48 rather than the intended 0.6, and **`.book-card.book-finished { opacity: 0.4 }` drags its own "✓ FINISHED" badge to 1.89:1**, so the one piece of text explaining the greyed-out state is the least readable thing on the card. The reward for progress is illegibility.

### 5.2 Focus indication is actively removed

`outline: none` on `.search-input:focus` (`:431`) and the two selects (`:528`), replaced only by a border shift to `#8b0000` at **1.43:1**. The focused state is less visible than the unfocused one. Buttons get no focus styling at all.

### 5.3 Theming is an override pile, not a token swap

Six tokens are swapped, then **44 `body.traitor-theme` rules patch what the swap breaks**. Consequences:

- **Semantic token names become lies**: `--imperial-gold: #cc0000`.
- **`--chaos-red` is byte-identical to the loyalist `--blood-red`** (`#8b0000`). Two names, one colour.
- **Elements that never theme**: `.status-badge.status-reading` stays bootstrap blue `#007bff` and `.status-finished` stays forest green `#228b22`. Web-safe blue and green are the two colours most alien to this art direction, and they are the only unthemed accents. `.mark-read-btn` becomes a red-to-olive-gold smear belonging to neither theme. `.spoiler-notice` renders pure red text on an orange wash. `.character-link:hover` gives purple text an orange halo.
- **4 of 11 tokens are declared and never referenced.**
- **196 hardcoded colour values against 153 token uses**, so 56% of colour decisions bypass the token layer. `rgba(212, 175, 55, ...)` appears at 6 distinct alphas and `rgba(139, 0, 0, ...)` at 8, none named, and every one breaks the theme swap.

### 5.4 Fonts

`@import` of Google Fonts creates a four-hop serial waterfall with no `preconnect` or `preload`. **Cinzel 400 is requested and effectively unused** (its only application is the hidden easter-egg heading). **`font-weight: bold` is applied 16 times to Share Tech Mono, which ships only a 400 weight**, so every one renders as synthesised faux bold at 0.65-0.85rem where smearing hurts most.

**No type scale**: 20 distinct `font-size` values with no ratio, including 0.5, 0.55, 0.6, 0.65rem steps. Live text at **8.8px** (`.status-badge` below 768px) and **9.6px** (`.progress-counter` below 480px). `body` sets no `line-height`, so all UI text inherits ~1.2.

**The blurb is justified monospace at ~87 characters per line** (`:1019`) with no hyphenation. Justifying a fixed-width font means inter-word gaps absorb whole character widths, producing visible rivers. The prose is the reason people visit and gets the least care on the page.

### 5.5 Layout and paint

- **Layout dead zone between 768px and ~1024px.** At 800px the container keeps `padding: 3rem` and the grid yields 2 enormous cards per row.
- **`body::before` and `.scanlines` paint the same horizontal gradient twice**, plus `.vignette` and `.dataslate-container::before`. Four stacked full-area non-composited overlays, and 1px-period gradients moiré on non-integer DPRs.
- **`.book-card::before` transitions `left`**, a layout property, on a card carrying a large background image. Should be `transform`.
- **`transition: all` used 14 times.** **Zero `prefers-reduced-motion` guards** against four infinite animations, including `.warning-strip` blinking perpetually with no pause control (WCAG 2.2.2). **Zero `@media (hover: hover)` guards**, so the card hover lift sticks after a tap on touch devices.
- **The title glitch reflows the `h1` six times in 600ms on every load** and may make screen readers announce a mangled string.
- **`.secret-button` at `z-index: 1500` sits above `.modal-overlay` at 1000**, so the 🔒 punches through every open book modal and is clickable.
- **`backdrop-filter: blur(5px)` behind 95-97% black** is pure GPU cost for no visible result.
- Scroll-lock is a raw `overflow: hidden` toggle in 12 places with no reference counting, no scroll restore and no scrollbar compensation, so opening any modal shifts the desktop layout ~15px.

### 5.6 Polish gaps

No empty state beyond a single amber line. No loading state, so the grid is blank until `window.load`. No disabled states. `:active` on exactly one control. No broken-image handling. **No `@media print`**, on a reading-order guide people will obviously want to print, and today printing yields a clipped 70vh inner scroller. No `<noscript>`, so a script error presents a full working header and footer over blank space.

**Returning traitor users see a full loyalist-gold header before the class is applied**, and 313 KB of aquila downloads and paints before being replaced by a 1.7 KB SVG.

### 5.7 Art direction

The conceit is right and the execution is one layer too literal. What works: the recovered-dataslate frame is followed through properly, Cinzel plus Share Tech Mono is an on-brief pairing, and `CLASSIFICATION: VERMILLION` flipping to `HERETICUS EXTREMIS` is real world-building rather than decoration.

What falls short:

1. **The grimdark is applied as filters, not materials.** Scanlines, vignette and glow over a plain dark-grey card grid. Real 40K art direction comes from stamped metal, wax seals, embossed aquilae, redacted blocks, riveted corners. Effects say "CRT". Materials say "ten-thousand-year-old artefact".
2. **Everything glows equally, so nothing is emphasised.** 35 `box-shadow` declarations plus text-shadow on nearly every coloured element flattens the hierarchy. Pick two things to glow.
3. **The Chaos theme is a hue rotation, not a faction.** Chaos is asymmetry and corruption of the original form, not "the Imperium but red". Currently the corruption reads as a broken stylesheet, and the illegible titles make that literal.
4. **The covers are the biggest missed opportunity.** The site owns 18 MB of the franchise's strongest illustration and shows it at 184px inside a 2px border. The art is the product and is currently the smallest element on the page.

Recommendations: let the covers lead with a hero tier; replace the CRT scanline with letterpress grain and stamped-plate edges; put the legion's heraldic colour and numeral on each card as a 3px left edge, which also fixes the colour-alone gap and makes the legion filter visually legible; make the traitor theme change surfaces and structure rather than recolouring text; **render spoiler-hidden passages as black redaction bars reading `[REDACTED BY ORDER OF THE INQUISITION]` that reveal on click**, turning an accessibility toggle into the best world-building on the site; retire the emoji for a small inline SVG set that themes via `currentColor`.

## 8. The second view: Daunt's Horus Heresy Timeline

### 8.1 What the chart is

Not a list. A **directed acyclic graph** of reading prerequisites, organised into legion swimlanes.

- A **main arc spine** across the top: Horus Rising → False Gods → Galaxy in Flames → Flight of the Eisenstein.
- **Vertical columns**, most headed by a Legion shoulder-pad icon, each carrying that Legion's storyline downward.
- **Node colour = faction. Node shape = format** (novel, novella, short story, graphic novel, audio drama, exclusive unnumbered short). **`(Book NN)` = the numbered anthology containing that story.**
- **Arrows = "read this before that"**, crossing columns wherever storylines converge, notably into *The Unremembered Empire*, *Vengeful Spirit* and *The Praetorian of Dorn*.

### 8.2 Extraction result

`daunt-chart.json` and `DAUNT-CHART-EXTRACTION.md` are now in the repo.

**185 nodes, 205 unique edges, 17 columns.** Formats: 101 short stories, 50 novels, 18 novellas, 12 audio dramas, 3 exclusive unnumbered shorts, 1 graphic novel. **71 of 205 edges cross columns.** 16 roots, 61 leaves, zero isolated nodes.

Confidence is high and the method is verifiable rather than eyeballed. `pdftocairo -svg` preserved draw.io's output almost losslessly, so nodes, colours, gradients and full path geometry came from the vector content stream. Two independent completeness checks came out clean: all 358 extracted text lines landed inside a shape (zero orphans), and all 209 arrowheads were consumed (205 arrows plus 4 folded-corner decorations). All 207 edge endpoints resolved to a node border at distance exactly 0pt.

Independently validated on receipt: **zero dangling edges and zero cycles**, so it is a usable DAG. 167 nodes high confidence, 18 medium.

### 8.3 Known gaps

1. **Novella and short story share an identical shape** (legend swatches match to within 0.01pt). The only differentiator is the presence of a `(Book N)` label, so all 18 `novella` nodes are inferred and flagged medium.
2. **Four lanes have no header icon** (`iron-warriors`, `terra-imperium`, `death-guard-garro`, `unaffiliated`). There are exactly 11 shoulder pads and all 29 embedded images were accounted for, so those names come from lane contents.
3. **Columns are not clean x-lanes.** The chart reuses vertical bands as the timeline descends: x 1490-1590 carries Sons of Horus material high and Terra material lower. **Colour is the reliable faction signal, not position.** `xRange` is an observed extent only.
4. **`unaffiliated` is a 41-node mixed bucket**, including a probable unlabelled Iron Hands / Shattered Legions lane at x 670-1030 that was not invented into a column.
5. Two icons unnamed with confidence: the dark red pad with a horned daemon head in gold flames at x 1296 (recorded as Word Bearers), and whether the Ultramarines omega pad heads the orange Imperium Secundus lane.

### 8.4 Reconciliation with `bookData`

- **161 of 185 chart nodes match** an entry (10 needed an alias).
- **24 chart nodes have no entry**: eBook novellas, Siege of Terra novellas, the four Tallarn component novellas, the event-exclusive *The Unburdened* and *The Honoured*, two anthology container nodes, and *Macragge's Honour*.
- **64 entries are absent from the chart**, dominated by the entire 17-book Primarchs series and by anthology shorts, notably **all 8 Mark of Calth stories**, consistent with Mark of Calth being missing from the chart's own anthology reference panel.
- **8 title typos exist in the chart itself** ("Tallarn: SIren", "Vulcan Lives"), preserved verbatim in `label`.

The chart is v0.9 of a community document, so it is a strong secondary source rather than an authority. Where it disagrees with `bookData`, neither is automatically right.

### 8.5 Attribution

The chart is a community work credited to "Daunt". Reproducing its structure and editorial judgement on a public site requires visible credit and a link to the original, and ideally the author's blessing.
