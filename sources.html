Sources and corrections

The Archive is an independent reading guide. Its catalogue currently has 228 entries representing 226 distinct works. Two works appear twice as reprints. The count describes records in this site, not every Horus Heresy publication.

How to read the record

The event atlas begins with fifteen arcs named in a Warhammer Community overview. Its work relationships are sourced, selected connections rather than exhaustive event bibliographies.

Recommended reading order is editorial advice. The chronological index uses estimated in-universe placement and should not be treated as a precise official timeline. Storyline Chart relationships are derived from Daunt's chart, with a separate ordering log for the Archive's chronology decisions.

Each work's detail panel lists the research links recorded for its summary. A link there does not mean that every displayed fact has been independently checked against that source. At this stage, 30 of the 228 entries include a Black Library or Warhammer Community link in their summary research. Separately, 13 anthology contents pages covering 143 entries have been checked against Black Library for titles, authors and membership. Garro is a novelisation of earlier works, and Mark of Calth has a disputed contents list. The remaining summary claims still require publisher-level verification. A date next to research links is the date that record was made, not a claim of current shop availability.

The titles, authors and novel format of the 12 Core works were checked against Warhammer Community's Horus Heresy Saga list on 26 September 2026. Title and author were also checked for 18 other works against its series overview, which explicitly calls Fury of Magnus a novella. Forty-seven further works have Black Library publication checks, including all 17 standalone Primarchs novels and seven earlier Garro works. The Mark of Calth product page confirms titles and authors for seven listed stories, bringing publisher coverage to 227 of 228 entries. Its omission of Athame remains flagged, with a separate bibliographic record for that story. The Tallarn page uses “novel” for its collected volume, but the Archive keeps it under Anthology because it combines four separately published works. In-universe dates and summaries still require separate review.

The 17 standalone Primarchs books are classified as novels using Black Library's novel catalogue and their individual product listings. These are distinct from the four novellas in the numbered The Primarchs anthology.

Character profiles list only works whose catalogue “Main Characters” field names that character exactly. These links are a structured extraction from inherited catalogue text, not an exhaustive or independently verified appearance index.

Seventy-four spoiler-free introductions, including all 65 novels and both anthologies, have been checked against individual Black Library product descriptions. These include all twelve Core works, all fourteen Siege entries and all seventeen standalone Primarchs novels. Several were rewritten to remove later plot turns or unsupported detail. Their work details cite the review source and warn that the external page may itself contain spoilers. The remaining novellas, short stories and audio dramas still need an editorial spoiler review.

Report a correction

Open a correction issue with the work title, the specific claim, a supporting source and whether the correction contains spoilers. You can also use the correction link in any work's detail panel, which includes its stable work ID.

Corrections are reviewed before publication. Substantive catalogue and ordering changes are recorded in the implementation log. This page and the log were last updated on 26 September 2026.
