SONS OF THE SELENAR

Author: Graham McNeill
Format: Novella

The battered Shattered Legions crew of the Sisypheum reach the Sol System divided, torn between a suicidal mission and orders to return to Terra and rejoin their Legion brothers. A strange signal pulls them down to embattled Luna and the secretive Selenar gene-cults, where their bonds of loyalty and devotion to one another are tested one final time.
