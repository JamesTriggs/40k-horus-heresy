THE DAMNATION OF PYTHOS

Author: David Annandale
Format: Novel

Survivors of the Drop Site Massacre from three shattered Legions regroup on an insignificant death world and carve out a refuge for human refugees adrift on the tides of the war. The jungle and its monstrous creatures are dangerous enough, but something far darker is gathering beneath the surface of Pythos.
