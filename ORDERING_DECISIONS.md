## Ordering Principles

1. **Strict in-universe chronology.** Books are ordered by their in-universe date, with no exceptions carved out for any series.
2. **Range handling.** A book covering a span (for example `007-009.M31`) sorts at the start of that span.
3. **Narrative present, not earliest content.** A book framed late that flashes back sorts at its frame, because that is when it can be read without being spoiled. This is why several of The Primarchs novels sort later than the events they depict.
4. **Simultaneous events.** Where two books share a date, the curated sequence is preserved: main novels first, then anthology stories, then character series.

Principle 1 replaces an earlier principle that placed pre-Heresy books at the end as supplemental material. That contradicted strict chronology, and the two could not both hold.
